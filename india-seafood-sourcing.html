Key Coastal Procurement Corridors Across India

Every coastal state in India offers unique raw-material advantages, seasonal availability, and specialized processing ecosystems. We connect your procurement requirements with the ideal production facility.

PRIMARY HUB • EAST COAST

Andhra Pradesh (Kakinada, Bhimavaram, Vizag)

Status: The epicentre of global Vannamei shrimp aquaculture, accounting for over 70% of India's shrimp exports.

Infrastructure: Deepwater port at Kakinada, container terminals at Visakhapatnam & Krishnapatnam, hundreds of modern EU & US FDA approved IQF spiral freezing facilities.

Core Species: Vannamei Shrimp (all sizes from 16/20 to 71/90), Black Tiger Shrimp (Penaeus monodon), value-added cooked and skewered shrimp.

Source Andhra Shrimp
WEST COAST HUB

Gujarat (Veraval, Porbandar, Mangrol)

Status: India's largest marine landing state, fronting the Arabian Sea with rich demersal and pelagic fishing grounds.

Infrastructure: Extensive blast and plate freezing facilities, direct reefer stuffing near Pipavav, Mundra, and Kandla ports.

Core Species: Ribbonfish (Trichiurus lepturus), Cuttlefish (whole, whole cleaned), Loligo Squid, Indian Mackerel, Croaker, Reef Cod.

Source Gujarat Marine Fish
SOUTHWEST COAST HUB

Kerala (Cochin, Neendakara, Kollam)

Status: The traditional birthplace of India's seafood processing sector, home to the Marine Products Export Development Authority (MPEDA).

Infrastructure: Cochin Port ICTT Vallarpadam, cutting-edge sashimi-grade tuna processing plants, value-addition expertise.

Core Species: Yellowfin Tuna (Thunnus albacares) fresh/frozen loins, Skipjack Tuna, Deep Sea Prawns, Karikkadi (Parapenaeopsis stylifera), Poovalan.

Source Kerala Tuna & Catch
NORTHEAST BAY OF BENGAL

Odisha & West Bengal (Paradeep, Kolkata)

Status: Renowned for high-salinity estuarine ecosystems and natural, premium wild & cultured Black Tiger shrimp.

Infrastructure: Paradeep deepwater port, Haldia and Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Kolkata, dedicated Black Tiger processing units.

Core Species: Giant Black Tiger Shrimp (Jumbo sizes 6/8, 8/12, 13/15 HOSO), Freshwater Scampi (Macrobrachium rosenbergii), Hilsa, Pomfret.

Source Black Tiger Shrimp
SOUTHEAST COROMANDEL COAST

Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Tuticorin, Cuddalore)

Status: Major marine biodiversity zone encompassing the Gulf of Mannar and Palk Strait.

Infrastructure: Two major container ports (Chennai Port and V.O. Chidambaranar Port Tuticorin) offering direct transshipment calls.

Core Species: Blue Swimming Crab (pasteurised & frozen meat), Sea White shrimp, Vannamei shrimp, Squid, Octopus.

Source Tamil Nadu Seafood
CENTRAL WEST COAST

Maharashtra & Karnataka (JNPT Mumbai, Mangalore)

Status: Gateway logistics corridor powered by India's premier container transshipment hub at Nhava Sheva (JNPT).

Infrastructure: Fast connection to global mainline vessels, specialized temperature-monitored reefer freight depots.

Core Species: Seer Fish (Kingfish), Pomfret (Silver & Black), Indian Salmon, Trevally, Dried & Salted seafood formats.

Source Western Catch

All India Seafood Sourcing FAQs

Practical answers for domestic and international seafood procurement professionals.

Does Mahadev Marine coordinate sourcing for domestic buyers in India as well?

Yes. In addition to global exports, we support large-scale domestic seafood processors, national hypermarket chains, and institutional distributors within India seeking consistent bulk Vannamei, Black Tiger, and frozen fish from certified coastal facilities.

How are seasonal seafood cycles managed across Indian states?

India’s seasonal fishing bans alternate between the East Coast (April 15 to June 14) and West Coast (June 1 to July 31). Because Mahadev Marine operates across both coasts, we provide year-round supply continuity by switching procurement between marine landings and year-round aquaculture harvest cycles.

Can buyers send their own inspection team to the processing facility?

Yes. We welcome buyer audits and third-party inspectors (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) at the processing facility. We coordinate security clearance, facility access, and sampling protocols in complete transparency.
