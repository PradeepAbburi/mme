Privacy policy.

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Last updated: July 2026.

Information collected

Mahadev Marine Exports may collect details you voluntarily submit, including your name, company, destination country, email, phone number, product specifications and message details.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to commercial seafood enquiries, prepare customized export quotations, verify product availability, coordinate documentation and maintain legitimate business records.

Sharing

Relevant enquiry information may be shared with processing partners, cold-storage facilities, logistics providers, or export compliance desks when required to evaluate or execute your export request. Information is never sold as consumer marketing data.

Website hosting

If this website is hosted on Netlify or another hosting infrastructure, form and technical telemetry may be processed under that provider’s standard security and operational practices.
