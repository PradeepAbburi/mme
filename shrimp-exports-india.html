Species Architecture

Vannamei vs. Black Tiger Shrimp Sourcing

Both cultured species thrive in the pristine estuarine waters of Andhra Pradesh and coastal India, offering distinct culinary and commercial advantages for global buyers.

Vannamei Shrimp (Litopenaeus vannamei)

Pacific White Shrimp represents over 90% of India's commercial aquaculture output. Celebrated for its naturally sweet taste, firm and crisp texture, clean white-pink cooked hue, and consistent year-round supply.

  • ✓ Count Range: 16/20, 21/25, 26/30, 31/40, 41/50, 51/60, 61/70, 71/90, 91/110 pcs/lb.
  • ✓ Freezing Methods: IQF (Spiral Freezers), Semi-IQF, Block Frozen.
  • ✓ Applications: Retail polybags, foodservice catering, value-addition ready-to-cook items.
  • ✓ Primary Markets: USA, EU, China, Japan, Middle East, Australia.

Black Tiger Shrimp (Penaeus monodon)

The prestigious Giant Tiger Prawn is favored in high-end culinary establishments and Japanese sashimi dining for its striking dark tiger stripes, meaty lobster-like firmness, and robust savory flavour profile.

  • ✓ Applications: Premium white-tablecloth restaurants, seafood steakhouse grilling, Japanese sushi.
  • ✓ Primary Markets: Japan, Western Europe (Spain, France, Italy), UK, USA.

Processing Formats

Available Preparation Styles & Cuts

Processed in modern HACCP, US FDA, and BRCGS certified facilities equipped with Japanese peeling lines, automated optical grading, and cryogenic IQF spiral freezers.

Whole Shrimp

Head-On Shell-On (HOSO)

Intact whole shrimp, sorted for uniform antennae, firm heads, and pristine appearance. Available in semi-IQF or brine-frozen 1kg, 2kg, and 4kg packs.

Block Frozen Classic

Headless Shell-On (HLSO)

Traditional high-volume export format. Head removed, shell intact. Block frozen in contact plate freezers (6 x 1.8kg or 6 x 2.0kg) with precise net weight counts.

Retail Ready

Easy Peel (EZ Peel)

Headless shell-on with a deep dorsal knife cut along the back and vein removed. Quick peeling convenience for consumers while maintaining grilled shell flavor.

Foodservice Core

PD & PDTO (Peeled Deveined)

Available as Tail-Off (PD) or Tail-On (PDTO). Cleanly deveined, individually quick frozen (IQF). 100% net weight or customized protective glaze.

Ready-to-Eat

Cooked Tail-On & Tail-Off

Cooked under computerised steam chambers to preserve juiciness, vibrant coral redness, and firm texture. Rapidly chilled and IQF frozen with zero pathogen tolerance.

Value Added

Butterfly & Shrimp Skewers

Precision butterfly cuts for tempura or breading, alongside bamboo wooden skewers (3 to 5 pieces per stick) designed for retail freezer cases and restaurant grills.

Shrimp FAQs

Common Buyer Questions on Indian Shrimp

Essential information on testing standards, treatment options, and shipment handling.

Do you offer both chemical-free (non-treated) and moisture-treated shrimp?

Yes. Depending on destination regulations and buyer specifications, we provide completely chemical-free / untreated shrimp (common in European organic and premium retail channels) as well as sodium tripolyphosphate (STPP) or non-phosphate moisture retention treated shrimp (common in US and Asian foodservice for moisture stability during thawing and cooking).

How is antibiotic residue testing guaranteed before shipment?

We enforce a strict multi-tier testing protocol: 1) Pre-harvest pond screening via ELISA, 2) Factory intake screening upon arrival, and 3) Final pre-export composite lot testing via Liquid Chromatography-Tandem Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) at accredited Export Inspection Agency (EIA) labs. We ensure zero tolerance for Chloramphenicol, Nitrofurans, Tetracyclines, and Fluoroquinolones.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for frozen shrimp exports?

Standard minimum order is 1 x 40ft High Cube refrigerated container (approx. 24,000 kg net weight). For initial commercial trials or mixed multi-species orders, 1 x 20ft reefer container (approx. 11,000 kg) can be coordinated.
