Quality, traceability and process discipline across every shipment.

Quality is carefully controlled at every stage, beginning with raw-material selection and continuing through processing, freezing, packing, storage and shipment.

Processing excellence

Modern export-approved seafood processing support.

Our products are processed through modern, export-approved seafood processing facilities equipped with advanced machinery, hygienic production systems, cold-storage infrastructure and stringent quality-control procedures.

The associated processing facility in Unguturu, Andhra Pradesh, has an installed shrimp processing capacity of approximately 7,300 metric tonnes per annum.

The facility is supported by advanced Individual Quick Freezing systems, block-freezing equipment, temperature-controlled storage and organised production lines designed to preserve the natural freshness, texture, flavour and nutritional value of every product.

Our processing capabilities include raw shrimp, cooked shrimp, individually quick-frozen products, block-frozen products, customised glazing, grading, packing and private-label production.

Quality Assurance

Our quality-control procedures include raw-material inspection, size and count verification, organoleptic evaluation, temperature monitoring, glaze verification, net-weight checking, metal detection, microbiological testing, packaging inspection and container hygiene verification.

Cold-Chain Management

Maintaining the cold chain is essential to protecting seafood quality. Products are handled under controlled temperatures throughout processing, freezing, storage, container loading and transportation.

Integrated sub-zero cold-storage systems and continuous temperature monitoring help preserve freshness and maintain product quality until the shipment reaches its destination.

Traceability & Compliance

Every order is processed according to the agreed buyer specification and applicable destination-market requirements. Our traceability systems help maintain complete production records from sourcing and processing to packing and final shipment.
