RFQ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on contracting, pricing, and shipment execution.

How are commercial prices quoted?

Prices are quoted in US Dollars (USD) per kilogram or per pound based on agreed incoterms (FOB Indian port, CFR destination port, or CIF with full marine cargo insurance). We provide transparent pricing reflecting real-time farm-gate raw material auctions and processing costs.

Can buyers request pre-shipment third-party inspections?

Yes. International buyers are encouraged to engage independent inspection agencies (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) at the processing facility. We coordinate complete access and sampling support for your chosen inspectors.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to container departure?

Typical lead time for standard export orders is 14 to 21 days from commercial confirmation and L/C issuance. This includes fresh harvest intake, processing, blast freezing, lab testing clearance, and port stuffing.
