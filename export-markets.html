Buyer Questions

International Seafood Export FAQs

Key facts and trade procedures for overseas importers partnering with Mahadev Marine Exports.

Can international buyers specify their own private label and carton artwork?

Yes. We coordinate OEM private label packaging including printed polybags, rider cards, master cartons with buyer barcodes, multilingual nutritional fact panels, and language-specific legal warnings required by destination authorities.

How does Mahadev Marine verify quality and specifications before cargo departure?

Our quality coordination team conducts pre-shipment inspections at the processing facility. We verify sensory freshness, temperature, piece count per pound/kg, glaze retention, net drained weight, inner vacuum integrity, carton drop resistance, and coordinate independent lab testing for antibiotic residue (LC-MS/MS) and microbiological pathogens.

What are the standard payment terms for export shipments?

Standard international trade terms include Irrevocable Letter of Credit at Sight (L/C at Sight) issued by prime international banks, or Telegraphic Transfer (T/T with advance deposit and balance against copy of shipping documents and Bill of Lading).

Do you arrange temperature data loggers inside the reefer container?

Yes. Every refrigerated container shipment can be equipped with calibrated digital USB/cellular temperature data loggers placed inside the cargo stow to continuously log cold-chain data from factory stuffing to destination destuffing.
