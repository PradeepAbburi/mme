About Mahadev Marine Exports Pvt. Ltd.

Simplifying seafood procurement while reducing sourcing risk.

At Mahadev Marine Exports Private Limited, we specialize in connecting international seafood buyers with reliable Indian seafood processing facilities through professional sourcing, procurement support, quality coordination, export documentation, and logistics management.

We understand that successful seafood procurement requires far more than competitive pricing. International buyers need consistency, transparency, technical knowledge, reliable communication, and complete confidence that every shipment will meet their exact specifications.

That is why Mahadev Marine serves as a trusted sourcing and procurement partner, helping overseas importers simplify their purchasing process while reducing sourcing risks.

Whether you are looking for Vannamei Shrimp, Black Tiger Shrimp, value-added shrimp products, or other frozen seafood, our objective is to connect buyers with suitable processing facilities that meet their commercial, technical, and destination-market requirements.
